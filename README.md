# Brilliant Benius helper prompts

Seven copy-paste prompts for marketing and support, with Indian freelancers first.

This is a prompt library, not an installable Claude plugin or an automated marketing tool. It does not connect to your accounts, send messages or publish posts. No paid kit contents are included.

## How to use

1. Open a prompt from the list below.
2. Copy its text into an AI chat you already use, including Claude, or send it to your assistant in WhatsApp.
3. Replace every [BRACKET] with your details.
4. Check the output and approve it before sending or publishing.

No API keys, paid connectors or software installation are needed for these text prompts. Your chosen AI service may have its own costs and usage limits. Links cannot be read by every AI chat; paste the relevant page text when needed.

## Checks before use

- Context: India, Hyderabad, rupees, freelancers first. Use only claims you can prove. No fake prices, client stories or promises of viral or automatic results.
- Keep customer names, phone numbers, payment references and other private details out of pasted examples. Use placeholders.
- The challenge prompt describes lessons unlocking each morning. Confirm this matches the actual challenge before sending any follow-up. The five messages are a follow-up sequence, not seven lesson scripts.
- The support prompt contains Brilliant Benius-specific delivery and refund wording. Confirm the current policy and applicable consumer rules before use. Other businesses must replace those lines with their own terms; do not use a blanket refund statement to deny rights required by law.
- The SEO prompt's character counts are writing targets, not a promise of search rankings. Verify the final counts.
- If the data is too small or incomplete, say so. Check Hindi and Telugu lines with a native speaker.
